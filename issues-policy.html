Where He Stands

Issues & Policy Positions

Clear priorities, built on the concerns residents raise most: roads, sanitation, safety, education, healthcare, and opportunity for young people and women.

These are sample position statements aligned with the administration's stated priorities. Review and approve final wording before publishing.

A thriving local economy starts with support for the small businesses, traders and artisans who form the backbone of Amuwo-Odofin.

  • Access to micro-grants and low-interest support for small businesses
  • Modernised, fairly-regulated local markets
  • Skills-to-jobs pipelines linking training to real opportunities

Every child in Amuwo-Odofin deserves a safe, well-resourced place to learn.

  • Rehabilitation of public school infrastructure
  • Support for indigent students
  • Partnerships to improve learning environments and teacher welfare

Safety is the foundation of community development — and it takes coordination, not slogans.

  • Continued multi-agency collaboration with the Nigeria Police, LASTMA and state task forces
  • Improved street lighting in high-risk areas
  • Support for community policing and neighbourhood watch groups

Good roads, drainage and public spaces are the backbone of a functioning community.

  • Continued rehabilitation of intra-community roads, including the Mile 2–Agboju–Festac Link Bridge axis
  • Sustainable drainage systems to reduce flooding
  • Upgraded public markets and recreational spaces

Accessible, quality primary healthcare should be within reach of every resident.

  • Upgraded primary health centres across both LCDAs
  • Regular free medical outreach programmes
  • Support connecting vulnerable households to health insurance schemes

Investing in young people is investing in the future of Amuwo-Odofin.

  • Vocational and digital skills training
  • Grants and mentorship for youth-led businesses
  • Sports, recreation and talent-development programmes

An inclusive community protects and empowers its most vulnerable members — a value championed directly by Vice Chairman Maureen Chika Ashara's community work.

  • Economic empowerment programmes for women
  • Support services for persons with disabilities and the elderly
  • Welfare interventions for indigent families
