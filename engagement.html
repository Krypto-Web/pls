Public Hearings & Town Halls

Meeting Residents Where They Are

Ward-level town halls are held on a rolling basis across Oriade and Amuwo LCDAs, giving residents a direct line to raise concerns before decisions are made — not after.

  • ●Rotating town halls across the 14 wards
  • ●Public hearings ahead of major bye-laws
  • ●Community-specific meetings for urgent issues (e.g. flooding, security)
