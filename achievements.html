Infrastructure

11 Roads Rehabilitated in 11 Months

Since taking office, the administration has prioritised the roads residents raised most often on the campaign trail. Work has included the flag-off of Road 23 in FESTAC Town on 16 October 2025 — described by the Chairman as a vital commercial artery for traders and commuters — alongside palliative works along the Mile 2, Agboju and Festac Link Bridge axis to ease congestion and improve safety.

Drainage & Sanitation

Restoring Order to Our Streets

On 19 August 2025, the administration led a major environmental sanitation exercise across Festac Town, clearing illegal structures, roadside shanties and obstructive parking in partnership with LASTMA, the KAI Brigade, the Nigeria Police Force, the Lagos State Environmental Taskforce and the Amuwo-Odofin Environmental Taskforce. The exercise directly answered residents' most common concern during the 2025 campaign: bad roads and a dirty environment.

Drainage clearing continues ahead of each rainy season to reduce flooding risk in low-lying communities, alongside monthly environmental sanitation drives across all wards.

Awards & Recognition

Honoured for Grassroots Impact

2026

NUJ Lagos State Media Games Ambassador

In July 2026, the Nigeria Union of Journalists (NUJ), Lagos State Council, honoured Chairman Sanusi as a Media Games Ambassador ahead of the 2026 NUJ Media Games (5–10 October, Agege Stadium, Lagos). The award, presented at the Amuwo-Odofin Secretariat by Media Games Committee Chairman Mr Adeleye Ajayi, recognised the administration's work on infrastructure renewal, youth empowerment, healthcare, education, community development, vocational training and small business support.
