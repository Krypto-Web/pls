Key Contributions

Plenary & Public Interventions

“…your servant-leader chosen by the will of the people and entrusted with the mandate…” — Chairman Sanusi, Inaugural Address, July 2025

During deliberations on the Youth Empowerment and Skills Acquisition Fund Bill, Chairman Sanusi underscored the importance of equipping young residents with practical, job-ready skills rather than short-term relief. Describing Road 23 as a vital commercial artery for the community during its rehabilitation flag-off, he noted that most commercial transport in Festac Town depends on that single corridor — a reminder, he said, of why infrastructure work must be prioritised strategically, not just broadly.

Civic Education

How a Bill Becomes Law

1

Draft & Sponsorship

A bill is drafted and formally sponsored for introduction to the Legislative Council.

2

First & Second Reading

The bill's title is read, then debated in principle before referral to committee.

3

Committee & Public Hearing

A committee reviews the bill in detail, often gathering public input before reporting back.

4

Third Reading & Vote

Councillors debate the committee's report and vote on final passage.

5

Transmission

A passed bill is transmitted to the Executive Chairman for consideration.

6

Chairman's Assent

The Chairman signs the bill into a bye-law, or returns it with comments.

7

Publication

The new bye-law is published and communicated to residents and agencies.

8

Implementation

Relevant departments and agencies begin enforcement and delivery.
